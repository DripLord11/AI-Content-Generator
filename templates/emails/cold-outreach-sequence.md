## Tips for Using These Templates

1. **Research before sending** - Look at their LinkedIn, recent press, and website
2. **Personalize the {{placeholders}}** - Generic emails get ignored
3. **Keep subject lines short** - Under 50 characters works best
4. **Send at optimal times** - Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM local time
5. **Track opens and clicks** - Use a tool like Lemlist, Apollo, or HubSpot
6. **A/B test subject lines** - Try different angles to see what resonates
7. **Follow up consistently** - Most deals happen after 5+ touchpoints

## Objection Handling Snippets

**"We don't have enough customer interviews"**
