## Pricing Strategy

### Recommended Packages

**Essential - $299**
- Best for: Clients testing your service
- Includes: Case study + 5 social posts + email blurb
- Your cost: ~$1
- Time investment: 30 mins

**Professional - $599** (Most Popular)
- Best for: Most B2B companies
- Includes: Everything + quote graphics + video script + blog post
- Your cost: ~$2-3
- Time investment: 45 mins

**Premium - $999**
- Best for: Enterprise clients, agencies
- Includes: Everything + sales slides + rush delivery + revisions
- Your cost: ~$4
- Time investment: 1 hour

### Pricing Psychology

1. **Always present three options** - Most people choose the middle
2. **Highlight "Most Popular"** - Social proof drives decisions
3. **Anchor with premium** - Makes professional seem reasonable
4. **Never discount the first sale** - Sets bad expectations

### When to Adjust Pricing

**Raise prices when:**
- You're closing more than 50% of leads
- You have more demand than capacity
- Clients never push back on price

**Consider discounts for:**
- Volume commitments (3+ case studies)
- Annual retainers
- Referral incentives

## Finding Clients

### Ideal Customer Profile

**Best fits:**
- B2B SaaS companies (always need testimonials)
- Marketing agencies (resell your service)
- Coaches and consultants (need social proof)
- Professional services firms (credibility is key)

**Company size sweet spot:**
- 10-500 employees
- Has marketing team or outsources marketing
- Active on LinkedIn
- Has existing customers to interview

### Lead Generation Channels

#### 1. LinkedIn Outreach (Highest ROI)

**Target titles:**
- VP/Director of Marketing
- Head of Content
- Demand Generation Manager
- Customer Marketing Manager

**Outreach script:**
> Hi {{name}}, I noticed {{company}} has been growing fast. Quick question: how long does it take your team to create case studies?
>
> We've built an AI that turns customer interviews into 10+ marketing assets Instantly. Curious if that's something that would help?

**Follow-up cadence:**
- Day 1: Initial message
- Day 3: Value-add follow-up
- Day 7: Case study example
- Day 14: Final touch

#### 2. Cold Email

Use the templates in `/templates/emails/cold-outreach-sequence.md`

**Tools to use:**
- Apollo.io (finding emails)
- Lemlist or Instantly (sending campaigns)
- Calendly (booking calls)

**Key metrics to track:**
- Open rate (target: 40%+)
- Reply rate (target: 5%+)
- Meeting book rate (target: 2%+)

#### 3. Content Marketing

**LinkedIn posting strategy:**
- Post 3-5x per week
- Topics: case study tips, B2B marketing, customer success stories
- Include before/after examples
- Share client results (with permission)

**Blog/SEO keywords:**
- "how to write a case study"
- "case study template"
- "customer testimonial examples"
- "B2B case study examples"

#### 4. Partnerships

**Potential partners:**
- Marketing agencies (white-label your service)
- Customer success platforms
- CRM consultants
- Business coaches

**Partnership model:**
- They refer clients → 20% commission
- Or: wholesale pricing for resale

## Sales Process

### Discovery Call Structure (15-20 mins)

**1. Opening (2 mins)**
> Thanks for taking the time. Before I dive in, can you tell me what caught your attention about our service?

**2. Pain Discovery (5 mins)**
- How many case studies do you create per quarter?
- How long does each one take?
- Who's involved in the process?
- What happens if you don't have enough case studies?

**3. Solution Overview (5 mins)**
- Walk through the process
- Show example outputs
- Explain turnaround time

**4. Pricing & Next Steps (5 mins)**
- Present packages
- Handle objections
- Schedule start date

### Closing Techniques

**The Assumptive Close:**
> Great, sounds like Professional package is the right fit. Do you have a customer interview ready, or should we schedule the kickoff for next week?

**The Urgency Close:**
> We have 3 slots left this month for new clients. Would you like me to hold one for you?

**The ROI Close:**
> If this case study helps close even one deal faster, you've already made your money back 10x. Should we get started?

## Client Onboarding

### Onboarding Checklist

**Before kickoff:**
- [ ] Send welcome email with intake form link
- [ ] Confirm package and payment
- [ ] Share interview best practices guide
- [ ] Schedule check-in call if needed

**Information needed:**
- [ ] Customer interview (audio/video/transcript)
- [ ] Customer name and company
- [ ] Product/service used
- [ ] Key metrics to highlight
- [ ] Target audience
- [ ] Preferred tone
- [ ] Special requests

### Interview Best Practices (Share with Client)

**Questions to ask their customer:**
1. What was your situation before using [product]?
2. What problems were you trying to solve?
3. Why did you choose [product] over alternatives?
4. What was the implementation like?
5. What results have you achieved?
6. What would you tell someone considering [product]?
7. Any specific metrics you can share?

**Technical requirements:**
- Audio quality: Clear, minimal background noise
- Length: 15-45 minutes is ideal
- Format: MP3, MP4, WAV, or transcript

## Content Generation & Delivery

### Processing Workflow

**Step 1: Receive & Review (15 mins)**
1. Download interview file
2. Quick listen/scan for quality
3. Note any issues or gaps
4. Upload to generation system

**Step 2: Generate Content (5-10 mins)**
1. Submit through intake form or API
2. Wait for AI processing
3. Download raw outputs

**Step 3: Quality Check (20-30 mins)**
1. Review all generated content
2. Fix any factual errors
3. Adjust tone if needed
4. Ensure brand consistency
5. Check for awkward phrasing

**Step 4: Package & Deliver (10 mins)**
1. Organize files by type
2. Create ZIP package
3. Upload to delivery portal
4. Send notification email

### Delivery Best Practices

- **Deliver early when possible** - Under-promise, over-deliver
- **Include a personal note** - "Here's what I loved about this story..."
- **Highlight best pieces** - "The LinkedIn posts are particularly strong"
- **Suggest next steps** - "Would you like to schedule a strategy call?"

## Quality Control

### Content Review Checklist

**Accuracy:**
- [ ] Customer name spelled correctly
- [ ] Company name correct
- [ ] Metrics match interview
- [ ] No hallucinated claims

**Tone:**
- [ ] Matches requested style (professional/casual/technical)
- [ ] Consistent across all pieces
- [ ] Appropriate for target audience

**Completeness:**
- [ ] All package items included
- [ ] Files open correctly
- [ ] Images render properly
- [ ] Character counts within limits (social)

### When to Regenerate

Regenerate content if:
- Major factual errors
- Completely wrong tone
- Missing key information
- Client explicitly unhappy

Don't regenerate for:
- Minor tweaks (edit manually)
- Style preferences (adjust prompts for future)
- Subjective feedback (discuss with client)

- Handle everything yourself
- Focus on process refinement
- Build case study portfolio

- Hire VA for admin tasks

## Common Objections

### "It's too expensive"

> I understand budget is a consideration. Let me share some math: if your team spends 20 hours on a case study at an effective rate of $50/hour, that's $1,000 in internal cost. Our Professional package is $599 and delivers Instantly. Would seeing those numbers help make the case internally?

### "We already have a process"

> That's great! Many of our clients had existing processes too. They found that our AI handles the 80% of repetitive work, freeing their team to focus on strategy and relationships. Would you be open to trying it for one case study to compare?

### "AI content won't sound authentic"

> That's a common concern, and a fair one. The key is that we use YOUR customer's actual words and story. The AI extracts and organizes the content, but the authenticity comes from the real interview. Happy to show you an example.

### "We don't have customer interviews"

> We can help with that! We provide interview question templates, and many clients find their customers are happy to share their story. We also offer interview facilitation if you'd like us to handle the conversation.

### "I need to think about it"

> Of course. What specific questions or concerns can I address to help you make the decision? Is there anything I haven't covered that would be helpful to know?

### "Can you send more examples?"

> Absolutely. I'll send over three examples from companies similar to yours. While you review those, would it make sense to tentatively hold a spot for next week's production calendar? No commitment, but it ensures we have capacity.
